Reversal & reorg (C1.11)

Corrections are never edits -- an entry is reversed by a new entry negating every line, linked via reversal_of, and an entry may be reversed at most once. Report a deposit reorg

You report one fact -- the deposit is gone -- and the ledger decides what it means from the order's state. funded → reversed, back to quoted, no loss. dispatching / settled → the payout already left, so the loss is booked to expense:loss:reorg and the system halts. Any other state is refused.
